Family HealthPortal – Terms of Use and Data Practices

Offered by Martin Spilkin · Last updated October 2, 2026

Who can use it

Family HealthPortal is a private app for members of one family. Logins are created only by the owner. It is not offered to the public.

What it does

With your permission, given when you sign in to your health system's MyChart and click Allow, the app downloads a read-only copy of your own health records: results, visits, medications, problems, allergies, immunizations, clinical notes, implants and orders, including records your health system received from other organizations. It never changes anything in your health system's records.

Where your data is kept

Downloaded data is stored on a private server in the owner's home in the United States, not on a commercial cloud service. Data is kept until you ask for it to be deleted.

Who can see your data

  • You.
  • Family members you authorize.
  • Software tools you or the owner use to view or analyze your data, such as AI assistants.

What the app never does

  • It does not sell, rent or share your data with anyone else.
  • It does not use your data for advertising or research.

Your choices and rights

  • You choose how long access lasts when you sign in, and you can end it at any time in the portal (Connected accounts → Disconnect) or in your health system's MyChart (linked apps).
  • You can ask for a complete copy of the data stored about you.
  • You can ask for all data stored about you to be deleted. Data is kept after you stop using the app unless you ask for deletion.
  • The portal keeps a partial log of access (sign-ins and file views).

No medical advice

The app shows your records and general information about tests. It does not give medical advice; talk to your clinicians about any decision.
